    Machining Notes

    Test Project 1 from the WorldSkills 2009 competition in Calgary. Use Show bottom on the 3D model to see the underside.

    Key features (from the drawing)

    • Overall size about 95 × 95 × 40 mm
    • Threads: M30, M8 × 1.2 (2×, as written on the drawing), M6 × 1 (2×)
    • Fits: Ø10 H7 (2×)
    • General tolerance ±0.1
    • Material: aluminium
